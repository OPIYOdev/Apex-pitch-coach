# APEX Pitch Coach

> **"Stop pitching. Start closing."**

APEX is an elite, high-stakes pitch simulator designed for founders who refuse to settle for "good enough." Built on the psychological frameworks of Oren Klaff (*Pitch Anything*), Chris Voss (*Never Split the Difference*), and the rigorous standards of Y-Combinator, APEX doesn't just fix your grammar—it fixes your frame.

---

## 🚀 Core Modules

APEX is structured into six high-intensity modules designed to transform your communication from a request into a command:

1.  **The Pitch Lab (Arena):** Our core simulation engine. Paste your script, outline, or raw thoughts and receive a brutal, high-status analysis.
2.  **Voice-to-Text Integration:** Dictate your pitch in real-time. We capture the raw energy of your delivery before the AI deconstructs it.
3.  **Roleplay Simulator:** Engage in a live back-and-forth with an AI trained to simulate skeptical investors, aggressive buyers, or indifferent partners.
4.  **Tone & Emotion Mapping:** Visualise the emotional arc of your pitch. Identify where you lose authority and where you build genuine urgency.
5.  **The Elite Rewrite:** Every simulation provides a "High-Status Rewrite"—a version of your weakest point crafted to command the room.
6.  **The Ascent (Progression):** A 5-level journey from *Rookie* to *Elite*, tracking your growth as a founder through the APEX Elite Index.

## 📊 The APEX Scoring System

We don't use generic metrics. Every pitch is measured against the four pillars of high-stakes persuasion:

| Metric | Focus | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Frame** | Status & Authority | Do you own the room, or are you a supplicant? |
| **Hook** | The First 30s | Did you seize local attention immediately? |
| **Logic** | The Business Case | Is your traction and fit undeniable? |
| **Urgency** | Cost of Inaction | Why must they move *now*? |

## 🏔️ The Ascent: 5 Levels of Mastery

Your journey through APEX is tracked via the **Elite Index (XP)**. As you improve your scores, you unlock higher levels of founder maturity:

*   **Level 1: Rookie** — The Foundation. Goal: Nail your one-liner.
*   **Level 2: Contender** — The Challenger. Goal: Make them feel the pain.
*   **Level 3: Closer** — The Navigator. Goal: Master the high-status close.
*   **Level 4: Dealmaker** — The Authority. Goal: Command the terms.
*   **Level 5: Elite** — The Master. Goal: They repeat your pitch back to you.

## 🛠️ Technical Architecture

APEX is a modern, full-stack application built for speed, security, and scalability.

*   **Frontend:** Expo (React Native) with NativeWind (Tailwind CSS) for a seamless cross-platform experience.
*   **Backend:** Node.js with Express and tRPC for type-safe API communication.
*   **Database:** PostgreSQL managed via Drizzle ORM with a unified schema for Auth and Product domains.
*   **AI Engine:** Powered by **Grok-2** for deep, contextual pitch analysis and high-status coaching.
*   **Payments:** Direct **M-Pesa STK Push** integration for seamless token purchases.
*   **Deployment:** Optimized for **Vercel** with serverless function support.

## 🛠️ Development Setup

### Prerequisites
- Node.js (v18+)
- pnpm or npm
- PostgreSQL Database

### Installation
1. Clone the repository: `gh repo clone OPIYOdev/Apex-pitch-coach`
2. Install dependencies: `pnpm install`
3. Configure environment variables (see `.env.example`)
4. Push the schema: `pnpm drizzle-kit push`
5. Start the development server: `pnpm dev`
